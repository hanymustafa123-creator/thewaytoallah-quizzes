Letters with Fatha

الحَرَكَات: الحُرُوف بِحَرَكَةِ الفَتْحَة

Unit 1, Lesson 1 · Now that we know the 29 letters, let's learn our first harakah (vowel mark): the Fatha. Each letter gets a friendly example word to help it stick!

What is a Harakah? A harakah (حَرَكَة) is a small mark placed above or below a letter that tells us which short vowel sound to add. The Fatha (فَتْحَة) is a small diagonal stroke ــَ placed above a letter — it gives the letter a short "a" sound, like in "cat".

حَرَكَة
Harakah (Vowel Mark)A small symbol above or below a letter that adds a short vowel sound. Plural: حَرَكَات (Harakaat).
فَتْحَة
FathaA small diagonal stroke ( ـَ ) above a letter, giving it a short "a" sound.
مَفْتُوح
MaftuhA letter that is "opened" — meaning it carries a Fatha. Example: بَ is "Baa maftuha".
